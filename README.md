# 📖 Overview

Enterprise Autonomous AI Operating System (AIOS) is a next-generation Enterprise AI Platform that transforms business documents, enterprise knowledge, workflows, and operations into an autonomous digital workforce.

Unlike traditional OCR or RAG applications, AIOS provides a complete enterprise operating system for AI-powered business automation.

The platform combines:

- Enterprise Document Intelligence
- Hybrid Search
- Enterprise RAG
- Knowledge Graph
- Multi-Agent AI
- Visual Workflow Studio
- Human-in-the-loop Approvals
- Digital Workforce
- AI Governance
- Marketplace & SDKs
- Multi-Cloud Deployment
- Digital Twin Infrastructure
- FinOps & SRE Automation
